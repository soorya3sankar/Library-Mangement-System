About Bookhaven

What this system does, and how to reach the front desk.

What Bookhaven does

Bookhaven is a small library management system built to replace paper registers. It keeps a catalogue of every title on the shelf, a list of enrolled members, and a running log of what's been issued and returned.

Everything is stored in the browser, so the catalogue, members and loan history you see are kept on this device between visits.

Borrowing rules

  • Loan period is 14 days from the date a book is issued.
  • Members can hold multiple titles at once, subject to copies on hand.
  • Overdue books are flagged automatically on the Issue & Return desk.
